# Dashboard Surface Brief

## Scope and mode

- Primary target: `app/(app)/dashboard/page.tsx`
- Mode: Operate
- This is the first expression of **The Kinetic Project Studio** replacement world.

## Audience, job, and success

PMO Officers use the Dashboard to identify the longest-waiting work, see who holds the Ball, inspect honest KRA/KPI definitions, and start a Progress or Bump update. Success means ownership, Stage, age, and the next useful action are legible within seconds while the application feels joyful enough to invite daily use.

## Approved composition

Use the **Studio Instrument Wall** composition. Mount a large warm-cream workbench canvas over the violet application atmosphere. Place the oversized greeting and time context at the top, then a horizontal longest-waiting strip. Use clean, regularly shaped KRA/KPI panels in the middle; preserve the asymmetric rhythm through size, spacing, typography, and color rather than novelty silhouettes. Place Latest Progress as the wider dense record area and Latest Bumps as a tighter companion rail.

North-star image: `docs/design/dashboard-instrument-wall-concept.png`. Preserve its hierarchy and atmosphere, but replace its novelty-shaped middle panels with the regular panel rule above.

Dock a dark technical Ball module on the right. It shows an original 3D basketball, the written current owner and group, held duration, and recent transfer context. Keep the Ball important but smaller than the data workspace. A visible Bump control uses the written label and an original sculpted 3D fist.

## Memorable moment and motion

The Ball is the memorable object. A transfer sends it through one short directional arc and settles it with a restrained spring. The Bump control anticipates slightly, then a second fist enters for one quick contact-and-rebound cycle. Do not loop either animation. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, use immediate state changes or a brief cross-fade with complete written confirmation.

## Constraints

- Preserve the real Dashboard content and never invent KPI results.
- Keep actionable records visible in the first viewport on common laptop sizes.
- Project rows preserve Code, name with State/Priority, Stage, Ball Owner, and held duration.
- Use regular rectangular or softly rounded KRA/KPI panels; no skewed, clipped, polygonal, or novelty-shaped cards.
- Dimensional objects never replace labels, familiar controls, status text, or navigation icons.
- Match the supplied Charm reference's level of craft and exuberance without copying its identity, characters, layout, or assets.
- Mobile linearizes the workbench and places written ownership before the Ball artwork.

## Open implementation decisions

- Final type families and exact palette values require live rendering and accessibility testing.
- Choose the lightest production medium that preserves the approved 3D quality: optimized raster/WebP sequences, CSS-assisted sprites, or a bounded canvas scene.
- The approved concept image is a north star for hierarchy and atmosphere, not a screenshot to trace literally.
